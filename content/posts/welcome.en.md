# Welcome

Hi. I'm Umut Can AKÇAY, a computer engineering student and full-stack developer based in İstanbul. This blog is where I write about software engineering, mathematics, and long-form thinking.

## What I write about

- **Engineering notes:** systems, networks, tools, and answers to "how does this actually work?" Posts that dig into the mechanism rather than skim the surface.
- **Mathematics:** concepts, proofs, and intuition. Ideas over formulas.
- **Thinking about AI:** agents, delegated authority, trust, and institutional reality. Calm analysis, not breaking news.
- **Long-form essays:** pieces written slowly enough to be worth re-reading months later.
